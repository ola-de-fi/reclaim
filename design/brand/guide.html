Reclaim · Brand guide · v1 · usereclaim.xyz

A lost-property office
for money.

Reclaim finds crypto people left behind on Robinhood Chain and helps them get it back. The brand borrows from the places that already do this for physical things: claim tags and tickets, status stamps, ruled ledgers, a calm clerk who tells you exactly what's on file. It should feel trustworthy, precise and a little warm. Never hype, never casino.

01

The claim tag.

A luggage tag on a string, tilted −10°, with a stamp-red return arrow: "this belongs to someone, send it back." It's the label a lost-property office ties to everything it holds, which is exactly what Reclaim does for stuck funds.

Small sizes

Below 48px use the small mark: no string, heavier lines. It's the favicon and app icon.

Clear space

Keep empty space around the tag equal to the eyelet's diameter ×2. Minimum size with the string: 24px / 6mm.

Wordmark

"Reclaim" set in Instrument Serif, regular, tracking −1%. Ink on paper, paper on ink. Never red, never italic, never all caps.

Do

Keep the −10° tilt and the red arrow. They make it a claim, not a price tag.

Don't

Straighten it, flip the arrow, or put a $ / coin on the tag.

Don't

Add drop shadows, gradients, glows or outlines.

Don't

Use Robinhood's green, feather or wordmark anywhere near it.

02

Paper, ink and one red.

Colour carries meaning, not decoration. Red means "act on this" and is used sparingly: the claimable amount, the one primary button, the tag's arrow. Navy means "in transit". Everything else is paper and ink.

03

Three typefaces, three jobs.

Role Face Sample
Voice: headlines, the wordmark Instrument Serif 400 (+ italic for the red phrase) We found $477.86 with your name on it.
Record: every number, address, label, countdown IBM Plex Mono 400 / 600, tabular figures 0.202753 ETH · 0x380c…de79 · 2d 19h
Explanation: body copy IBM Plex Sans 400 / 600 Bridge withdrawals nobody claimed. Deposits that failed halfway.

Rule of thumb: if it's a fact on file, it's mono. If it's the clerk talking, it's serif. Micro-labels are mono, uppercase, tracked +12–14%.

Ruled paper

Faint horizontal rules (rule colour at ~33%) on paper for hero and social backgrounds. Never on top of dense data.

Hard shadows

3px offset ink shadow, no blur, on things you can press or that are "printed": tickets, the claim form, primary buttons.

Square corners

Radius 0–2px. Circles only for the stamp and avatars. No pill buttons, no glass, no gradients.

05

The clerk's voice.

Reclaim talks like a good lost-property clerk: calm, specific, on your side. It says exactly what's on file and what happens next. It never promises money it hasn't found, and it never says "unlock", "moon", "free money" or uses rocket emoji.

Specific, not hyped

✔ "0.2028 ETH is ready to claim on Ethereum."
✘ "You've got FREE MONEY waiting 🚀"

Honest when empty

✔ "Nothing unclaimed under this address. That's a good result."
✘ "Oops! No luck this time 😢"

Safety in plain words

✔ "No token approvals. You sign every transaction."
✘ "Military-grade security."
