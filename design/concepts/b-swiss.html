Your crypto
is stuck.
Get it back.

Reclaim scans any address for unclaimed bridge withdrawals and failed deposits between Robinhood Chain and Ethereum, then helps you recover them in one signed transaction.

No wallet needed to scan. Connect only when you’re ready to claim.

A
Non-custodial
Funds move from the bridge straight to your wallet. We never hold them.
B
Simulated first
Every claim is simulated before you sign.
C
No approvals
Reclaim never asks for token approvals.
D
No fee
You pay network gas. Nothing else.
B / 02
How it works + what it finds
numbered, flush-left
01

Three steps.
No custody.

Reading the chain is free and needs no signature. You sign only when there is something to recover.

1.1

Paste an address

Any Ethereum address. The scan is read-only — nothing to connect, nothing to sign.

1.2

Three detectors run

Withdrawals, failed deposits and wallet balances are checked in parallel across both chains.

1.3

Review, simulate, sign

Each claim shows the contract called, where funds go, and a passed simulation before you sign.

02

What it finds

Only the first two count toward “recovered.” Balances are shown for context.

A

Unclaimed withdrawals
Robinhood Chain → Ethereum

Bridge withdrawals sit in a ~6.4-day challenge period, then wait for you to claim them on Ethereum. Many never get claimed.
Claimable now Pending
B

Failed deposits
Ethereum → Robinhood Chain

A deposit whose auto-redeem failed must be redeemed manually within 7 days. After that, it is lost.
7-day deadline
C

Wallet balances

ETH, WETH, USDC, USDT, WBTC, LINK, UNI already in the wallet. Shown, but not counted as recovered.
