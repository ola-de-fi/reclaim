Three steps at the counter.

STEP 01

File an enquiry

Paste any address. We read Robinhood Chain and Ethereum. Nothing is signed, no wallet needed.

STEP 02

Check the register

Every item gets a ticket: what it is, where it's stuck, and whether it can be collected today.

Collect, in person

Connect your wallet, review a simulated claim, and sign it yourself. Funds go straight to you.

Notes on the system

  1. One stamp color. Vermilion means "act on this" (claimable, claim button). Navy means "in transit, wait". Ink-grey means "for the record".
  2. Serif speaks, mono counts. Every number, address and hash is set in Plex Mono so figures align and read as evidence.
  3. Tickets tear at the stub: the stub always holds the net amount and the single action, so the eye goes from item to value to button.
  4. Ruled paper, hard 1px borders and offset shadows replace rounded cards; corners stay at 2–3px.
  5. Ink on paper is 15.6:1; vermilion text (#A82A12) on paper is 6.4:1; navy is 10.8:1.
