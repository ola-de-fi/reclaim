02 How it works / what it finds

Three detectors. One pass. Nothing moves until you sign.

STEP 01

Paste an address

Any address on Robinhood Chain. The scan is read-only, so you don't need to connect a wallet to look.

STEP 02

Sweep the history

Each detector reads its own channel of bridge and chain data and pings when it finds something.

STEP 03

Review, then claim

Every claim is simulated first. You see the contract, where funds go and the gas before you sign.

CH-1 · Withdrawals

Unclaimed bridge withdrawals

Robinhood Chain → Ethereum withdrawals that were started but never finalized on Ethereum.

Claimable now, or pending through the ~6.4-day challenge period
CH-2 · Deposits

Failed deposits

Ethereum → Robinhood Chain deposits whose auto-redeem failed. These have to be redeemed manually.

Redeem within 7 days or the funds are lost
CH-3 · Balances

Wallet balances

Shown for context so you see the full picture. Not counted as recovered.

System notes

  • Brass means recoverable now. Only one brass button per screen.
  • Steel blue means pending, locked by the challenge period.
  • Grey is context, like wallet balances that aren't counted.
  • Mono readouts carry the coordinates (withdrawal #, block) so anyone can verify a ping on an explorer.
  • Motion is limited to the sweep arm and meters, and both stop under reduced motion.
